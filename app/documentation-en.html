User guide

3CXplore training, version 20.3.2: what each menu is for and how to use it. Menus follow the order of the simple menu; the full menu shows the same tabs flat.

Getting started

Open a backup
On the Dashboard (at the bottom of the page once a backup is open), click “Choose a backup (.zip)” or drag and drop the file. Processing happens entirely in your browser: nothing is sent to the Internet. The backup is remembered until “Close the backup”.
Guided tour
The guided tour presents the essentials in a few bubbles. Start it with “Guided tour” at the bottom of the left menu, in the Interface tab or with the command palette. To start it every time a backup is opened, turn on the “Guided tour when a backup is opened” switch under the “Choose a backup” button (off by default).
Choose a store
The “All stores” list at the top right limits every tab to one store. Go back to “All stores” to see the whole system.
The menu
The left menu is arranged in sections, each with its own colour: Every day (Home, Dashboard, To fix, Calls, Stores), I want to… (the wizards: add or remove an employee, open a store, prepare public holidays), My setup (people, extensions and lines, how calls travel, voicemail, security) and Understand (numbers and statistics, what changed). Click a section: its pages appear right below, like the stops of a metro line; the open page is the filled stop. At the top of each page, the breadcrumb (Home › section › page) shows where you are; on a phone and with the collapsed menu, the other pages of the section also appear as pills. This guide follows the same order. The menu is hidden at first: the “Menu” button, top left, opens it over the page (Esc closes it). To keep it always shown, choose “Always shown” in the Interface tab.
Home
The Home page opens first: the 3CXplore mascot asks you a question (“What do you want to do?”) and you answer by tapping a large button. In two or three answers, it opens the right page. “Go back” and “Start over” change your answer; below, a field finds a page by its name. To open another page at start-up, choose it in the Interface tab (Home tab).
Find a page
At the top of the menu, the “Find a page…” field (and the one on the home page) finds a page as you type a few letters of its name, ignoring accents and capitals. Each result shows its section. The arrow keys choose a result, Enter opens it and Esc clears the search.
Search
The search bar (“/” key) finds an extension, a MAC address, a DID, a name or a queue. The command palette (Ctrl+K, or Cmd+K on Mac) opens any tab, extension or store by typing a few letters.
Help on a tab
The “?” button next to each tab's title opens that tab's explanation in this guide.
Language
The FR / EN button in the top bar switches the interface between English and French; the guide opens in the same language.
Export and print
Most tables have an “Export (CSV)” button (opens in Excel); sheets and reports can be printed or saved as PDF. Exported files go to Downloads: delete them when you no longer need them.
Personal settings
Your notes, statuses, goals, important contacts, absences and preferences stay in your browser. Export them with “Settings backup” to find them elsewhere.
Updates
The download button in the top bar shows whether a new version of 3CXplore is available. The version history is in “Changelog”, at the very bottom of the left menu.
Privacy
Never share the backup .zip file: it contains calls, recordings, voicemails and system credentials. Passwords, PINs and keys are hidden in the tool.

Every day

Home

Home

Every day › Home

The opening page: an assistant. The 3CXplore mascot says how many things need fixing and asks what you want to do; you answer by tapping a large button (see calls, fix a problem, add or remove someone, see my store…) and, in two or three answers, it opens the right page.

How to use it
  • Tap the answer that fits: the next question appears below, until the page that answers it.
  • “Go back” changes the last answer, “Start over” returns to the first question. When you come back to Home, you find where you left off.
  • You can also type what you are looking for in the field at the bottom (for example “missed” or “holidays”), or open the full menu. The « Close backup » button next to it closes the open backup (its name is shown below) and erases what you choose from this browser.
Good to know: The 3CXplore logo, top left, always brings you back here.

Dashboard

Dashboard

Every day › Dashboard

The starting point: at the top, the health score, key figures for the system or the selected store and the three problems that lose the most calls; then the outcome of inbound calls, volume by day and by hour, the most active extensions and numbers; at the very bottom, the open backup and server information.

How to use it
  • First choose a backup (.zip): this is where it is opened. To change it, scroll to the bottom of the page; “System” expands there to show the FQDN, licence, paths and codecs.
  • Look at the outcome of inbound calls (answered, lost, voicemail, forwarded) to spot a service problem at a glance. The percentages are of customer calls: calls sent to another 3CX (bridge) are shown separately. Under each rate, the basis of the calculation is shown (for example “4 149 of 5 908 customer calls”).
  • Click an extension or a number to open its details.
Good to know: A banner flags important numbers waiting for a call-back and what is new in the backup compared with the previous one.

Fixes by user

Every day › To fix › Fixes by user

What needs fixing on each employee's extension, with what to do: disabled extension, voicemail, duplicates, parkings, BLF keys and external forwards. The list is the same as in the Employee sheet.

How to use it
  • Employees are ranked by number of important fixes; filter by topic or search for an employee.

  • Print the list for the technician (one page per employee if you wish) or export it to Excel.
Good to know: With the next backup, the list is recalculated: a fix made in the console disappears by itself.

Repeat callers

Every day › Calls › Repeat callers

Served calls followed by a new call from the same customer shortly after (24 h, 48 h or 7 days): often a problem not solved the first time. Rate by store, queue and agent, and the most recurring customers.

How to use it
  • Compare rates between stores, queues and agents rather than aiming for zero.
  • Add suppliers, partners or other sites to the exception numbers (or click “Exclude” on a row).
Good to know: Call-backs under 10 minutes and very frequent callers are left out by default; two boxes let you include them.

Number sheet

Every day › Calls › Number sheet

Everything the backup knows about a number, on a single page: whether it is an inbound number (DID) of the system, a caller, an extension or an employee's cell phone, with its calls, who answers, lost calls and callbacks.

How to use it
  • Type the number in any format ((555) 123-4567, 5551234567, +1…) or an extension number, then choose the period.
  • For a DID: store, trunk, inbound rule and destinations, calls received, answered, sent to voicemail and lost, who answers and the callers to call back on this number, with links to the diagram and the simulator.
  • For a caller: name in the phonebook, important number, stores contacted, calls received and made, missed calls and their callback by store, and a link to the full history.
  • The header search (« / » key) also offers the Number sheet when you type a phone number.
Good to know: From the list of callers to call back for a DID, clicking a number opens that caller's sheet directly.

Hold report by employee

Every day › Calls › Hold report by employee

A printable report on the calls an employee puts on hold (parking): how many are lost because the caller hangs up before being picked up, the month-by-month and week-by-week trend, the hardest days and hours, and the day-by-day detail to compare with their schedule.

How to use it
  • Open it with the « On hold » button of the Employee sheet, the « Hold report by employee » button of the Shared parkings tab, or the « Report » link in the table of employees who park the most calls.
  • Choose the employee (the list shows how many calls each one put on hold) and the period, including custom dates.
  • Print the report or save it as PDF; the Excel export gives the detail of every call put on hold.
Good to know: The « By day and time of day » table can be compared directly with the schedule: if losses follow a time slot rather than a person, it is a staffing or organisation issue.

The plan of shared parkings by store: you assign each store its parkings (SP0, SP1…), and the tool lists what does not follow the plan on the phones and in parked calls.

Voicemail boxes

My setup › Voicemail › Voicemail boxes

Each voicemail box (extensions, queues, groups): enabled or not, messages sent by email, greeting, messages still kept, last check and everything that leads to it.

How to use it

Glossary

The 3CX words used in the tool, explained simply. In the guide, terms underlined with dots lead here.

Agent
Extension that is a member of a queue; it must be logged into the queue to receive its calls. See: Agents, Agent occupancy, Key people
Answer rate
Share of inbound calls answered by a person (or forwarded outside and answered). See: Statistics, Targets, Health score
Audit log
List of changes made in the 3CX console and of logins, with the author, the time, the before and the after. See: Audit log, Changes, Employee history
Auto attendant (IVR)
Voice menu (“For service, press 1…”): a message, then a destination per key, and what happens if the caller chooses nothing. See: IVR menus, IVR menus
Backup
Backup .zip file produced by 3CX: configuration, call history, voicemails. 3CXplore reads it entirely in your browser. See: Dashboard, Comparison, Tracking
BLF key
Phone key with a light that shows whether a colleague is free, busy or ringing; pressing it calls them or picks up their call. See: BLF keys, Key template
Bridge
Link between two 3CX systems (for example two sites): calls can pass from one to the other. See: Trunks, Internal calls
Call history (CDR)
The detailed record of each call kept by 3CX (Call Detail Records): steps, durations, who answered. Most statistics come from it. See: Calls, Customer history
Call parking
Putting a call “on park” to pick it up from any phone (parking key or code). See: Shared parking, Parking plan
Caller ID
The number shown to the person called when an extension calls outside; it should be a DID of the right store. See: Duplicates and inconsistencies, Extensions
Connected, lost, abandoned call
Connected: a person answered. Lost: nobody answered (hung up in the menu, abandoned in a queue, no answer). Abandoned: the caller hung up before being answered. See: Statistics, Missed calls, Calls
Department
3CX group that brings extensions together, holds the hours (open, closed, break, holidays) and decides where calls go at each moment. See: Departments, Org chart, Hours
DID
Direct inward dialling number (in French SDA): a public phone number that rings in 3CX. Each DID has an inbound rule that decides where the call goes. See: Call flows, Call flow (diagram), Call simulator, DID check, Unused DIDs
Extension
A 3CX user, identified by a short number; it can have one or more phones and the 3CX app. See: Extensions, Phones, Licences and extensions
External forward
Sending a call to a number outside 3CX (mobile, another company); it goes through a trunk and may cost money. See: External forwards, Security
Firmware and template
Firmware is a phone's internal software; the template is the configuration model 3CX sends to the phone (provisioning). See: Phones
Forwarding profile (status)
An extension's status (Available, Away, Do not disturb…) and what happens to its calls in each case: no answer, busy, not registered. See: Extensions, External forwards
Inbound rule
For a DID, where calls go depending on the moment: office hours, out of hours, holidays. See: Call flows, Trunks
MAC address
A phone's unique identifier, written under the device; 3CX uses it to send the phone its configuration. See: Phones, Extensions
Office hours, out of hours, break, holidays
A department's four moments: each has its own destination (open menu, closed menu, break message, holiday message). See: Hours, Holidays, Departments
Outbound rule
Chooses which trunk a call leaves by, based on the number dialled (prefix, length), and can transform the digits. See: Outbound rules
Overflow
Destination used when a queue or group does not answer in time: voicemail, another queue, a menu, an external forward. See: Queues, Routing traps
Prompt (recorded message)
Audio file played to the caller: menu greeting, closed message, queue intro, music on hold. See: Voicemail & audio, IVR menus, Queues
Queue
Callers wait there for an agent to become free, with a greeting and music; agents ring according to a strategy, and the call overflows after a maximum wait. See: Queues, Queue calls, Agents
Ring group
Rings several extensions at once or in turn, with no waiting or music; if nobody answers, the call goes to a fallback destination. See: Ring groups
Ring strategy
How agents or members are rung: all at once, in turn, least busy, etc. See: Queues, Ring groups
Role
A person's rights in a department: user, manager, receptionist, etc.; they decide what the person sees and can do. See: Rights matrix, Visibility and rights, Compare two users
Service level
Share of a queue's calls answered within a target time (for example 20 seconds). See: Queues, Targets
Simultaneous calls (licence)
Number of external calls at the same time: the 3CX V20 licence sets the maximum. See: Licences and extensions
SIP provider and gateway
The SIP provider is the carrier that carries calls over the Internet; a gateway converts a traditional phone line for 3CX. See: Trunks
Store code
Prefix or code in names (departments, queues, extensions) that lets 3CXplore attach each element to a store; can be corrected in the Stores tab. See: Stores
Trunk
External line that connects 3CX to a telephone carrier (SIP provider) or a gateway. DIDs arrive through a trunk and outbound calls leave through it. See: Trunks, Outbound rules
Voicemail
Box where the caller leaves a message; messages can be sent by email. Queues and groups can also have a box. See: Voicemail boxes, Voicemail & audio
